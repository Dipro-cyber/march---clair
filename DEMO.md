# Marché Éclair in three minutes

Start the app and open it in English. Complete Lessons 1–5 in the demo browser ahead of time to unlock Lesson 6. Open Lesson 1 from the market map and leave Practice Mode active. Turn on Data Saver to run the demo with scripted dialogue.

## 0:00–0:30 · Problem

*Show the market stall on a narrow screen.*

“A vendor is selling mangoes and a customer says they paid on Lightning. There’s a queue, the phone signal is weak, and the customer is waving a screenshot. How does the vendor know the right amount actually arrived? Marché Éclair is for someone learning to accept their first Lightning payment on the phone they already use.”

## 0:30–1:45 · Solution

*In Lesson 1, select **Create Lightning Invoice** and **Show invoice to Amina (She pays)**. Point to the paid status and the increased play-sat balance, then open the two-question check. Return to **Home** and select Lesson 6 on the market map to show the wrong-amount scene. If Lesson 6 is still locked, tell the scam story while the Lesson 1 check is on screen.*

“You run the stall. In Practice Mode you create the invoice, then play through a customer payment. I can see the invoice change to paid and check the balance on my own screen. The two-question check helps me recall what happened. In the scam lesson, the customer’s screenshot shows 50 sats for a 500-sat sale. I refuse and verify the payment in my own wallet. The six lessons cover the mechanics of payments and the decisions a vendor makes at the counter.”

## 1:45–2:30 · Intended impact

*Point to Data Saver, then open the Real Mode dialog and show its test-network warning.*

“The game is available in French and English. Data Saver uses scripted text, and Practice Mode can run offline once the lesson assets are cached. We want learners to verify the amount and look for settlement in their own wallet before handing over the goods. Lesson 1 also lets them try receiving test sats if they have two test wallets. Today I’m showing the Practice flow.”

## 2:30–3:00 · Next steps

*Close the dialog and show the progress screen.*

“Next we’ll try this with first-time vendors on the phones and connections they use. We’ll track lessons completed, first-answer accuracy on the scam quiz, and drop-off by lesson. We’ll also watch whether learners can verify a payment on their own. That gives us a way to decide which lessons to improve first.”
